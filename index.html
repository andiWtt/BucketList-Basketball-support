Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Apple Watch?

Ensure your iPhone and Apple Watch are paired. Install the BucketList app on your Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. Open BucketList on both devices and start recording.

How do I use the app to track my shots?

1. Open the Recording tab and position your device to capture the basketball hoop. 2. Tap the red record button to start your session. 3. Make your shots - the app automatically detects and records each attempt. 4. Twist the Digital Crown on your Apple Watch to mark each shot (10s cooldown between marks). 5. Tap the stop button when finished. 6. View your shot clips, statistics, and performance analysis in the Games tab.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions, find BucketList Pro, and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions in the App Store app.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload any videos or statistics to cloud servers, ensuring your privacy and data security.

Can I export my shot videos?

Yes! Each shot clip can be shared or saved to your Photos library. Tap the share button on any video to export it.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free users can record 1 game per week. BucketList Pro unlocks unlimited recording, advanced statistics, multi-dimensional filtering, and priority support.

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

BucketList respects your privacy. All data, including videos, statistics, and personal information, is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers.

We collect minimal analytics data to improve app performance:
